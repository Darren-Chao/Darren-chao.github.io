About Me

Hi, I'm Darren.

I'm a designer and engineer currently pursuing a Master of Design Engineering at Brown University and Rhode Island School of Design. Before that, I studied Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at UC Berkeley.

I'm interested in creating thoughtful, human centered objects and experiences. Sometimes that means solving real problems. Sometimes it means making something playful, strange, or simply enjoyable to exist alongside. I care about work that makes life feel a little more intentional and a little more alive.

If this resonates with you, I'd love to connect.
